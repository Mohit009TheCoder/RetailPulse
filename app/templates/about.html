About RFM Analysis

Understanding customer segmentation through RFM methodology

What is RFM Analysis?

RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis is a marketing technique used to quantitatively rank and group customers based on the recency, frequency, and monetary value of their recent transactions to identify the best customers and perform targeted marketing campaigns.

Recency

How recently a customer has made a purchase. Customers who purchased recently are more likely to purchase again.

Frequency

How often a customer makes a purchase. Customers who purchase frequently are more engaged with your business.

Monetary

How much money a customer spends. Customers who spend more are more valuable to your business.

Customer Segments

Champions

Best customers who bought recently, buy often and spend the most. Reward them and make them advocates.

Loyal Customers

Customers who buy regularly with good monetary value. Upsell higher value products and engage them.

Potential Loyalists

Recent customers with average frequency. Offer membership or loyalty programs.

New Customers

Recent buyers with low frequency. Provide onboarding support and build relationships.

Promising

Recent buyers with good potential. Create brand awareness and offer free trials.

Need Attention

Below average customers. Make limited time offers and recommend products.

At Risk

Previously frequent buyers who haven't purchased recently. Send personalized reactivation campaigns.

Can't Lose Them

High value customers who haven't purchased recently. Win them back with renewals and helpful products.

Hibernating

Inactive customers with low engagement. Recreate brand value and offer relevant products.

How to Use This System

  1. Dashboard: View overall statistics and segment distribution
  2. Segments: Explore detailed information about each customer segment
  3. Customers: Search and filter individual customers by segment or ID
  4. Export: Download customer data for further analysis

Scoring System

Each customer receives a score from 1-5 for Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics:

  • 5: Best (most recent, most frequent, highest spending)
  • 4: Very Good
  • 3: Good
  • 2: Fair
  • 1: Needs Improvement

The RFM Score is the sum of these three scores, ranging from 3 to 15.
